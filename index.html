How It Works

Clear information to help you choose the right PT clinic for your needs and budget.

1

Search your area

Enter any US city or ZIP. We pull up PT clinics near you with live data — ratings, hours, and contact info.

2

Set your insurance info

Pick your plan, enter your deductible and out-of-pocket max. Everything stays in your browser — we don't store it.

3

Compare and track

See estimated costs with insurance vs cash for each clinic. Track the ones you're interested in to compare side by side.

4

Report what you paid

After your visit, report your actual cost. It's anonymous and helps improve estimates for everyone else.

📊 How Our Pricing Works

Getting accurate healthcare prices is hard — insurance companies don't make it easy. Here's how we build our estimates and how you can help make them better:

1
Published Rate Data

We start with publicly available insurance rate schedules and CMS data to build baseline estimates for each plan type.

2
Patient Price Reports

When patients report what they actually paid, our estimates get smarter. Every report improves accuracy for that clinic and similar ones nearby.

3
Clinic-Verified Prices

Clinics that claim their listing can set their actual cash rates and insurance contracts — marked with a ✓ Verified badge.

4
API Integration (Coming Soon)

We're working on integrating with healthcare pricing APIs like Turquoise Health to pull real negotiated rates directly from insurers.
